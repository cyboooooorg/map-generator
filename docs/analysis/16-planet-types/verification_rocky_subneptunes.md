## Summary of corrections for the report

1. IAU working definition: add the August 2018 mass-ratio condition M/M_central < 2/(25+sqrt(621)) ~ 0.04 alongside the 13 M_Jup limit (confirmed via arXiv:2203.09520).
2. Abe 2011: land planet inner limit 415 W/m2, aqua planet 330 W/m2 (not 375, which is a different paper, Leconte 2013); the 77% vs 90% outer-edge figures are right.
3. TOI-270 d radius: 2.1 R_E (Van Eylen 2021) or 2.2 R_E (Benneke 2024), mass 4.78 M_E.
4. Soter and Stern-Levison: bibliography confirmed, discriminant formulas still unverified; keep [M].
5. Rogers 2023 ApJL 947 L19 exists (Rogers, Schlichting, Owen; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/acc86f). Rogers 2024 (2409.17394) is a separate paper, accepted in ApJ.
6. Luque et al. 2025 (arXiv:2505.13407) is now published as A&A 700, A284.
7. "Kite 2016" does not exist in this form; the relevant papers are 2019-2020.
8. Hu et al. 2507.12622: no journal reference, "Submitted to AAS Journals" on the page opened; keep "préprint non publié" but recheck on the day of publication.
9. Confirmed unchanged: Kopparapu 2018 bins (now with edges), Borucki 2011 bins, Teske 2013 C/O 1.12 -> 0.78 +/- 0.08, Petigura 2013, Fressin 2013, Howard 2012, K2-18 b CH4/CO2/DMS numbers, GJ 1214 b, LHS 1140 b, GJ 9827 d.
