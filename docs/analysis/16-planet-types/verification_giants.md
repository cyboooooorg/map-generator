# Verification of the giants section

Scope: section "Géantes : de Neptune aux Jupiters ultra-chauds, et leurs frontières" of
`reports/Types de planètes connus.md`, plus the unverified items of `giant_planets.md`.
Checked on 2026-11-15 against arXiv abstract pages, ar5iv full text, Crossref and
search-engine records of ADS/journal pages. "Confirmed" means a source was opened or
returned in this session; nothing is marked from memory. Items not reached are listed
as "not verified" at the end.

## Corrections to make in the report

1. Sudarsky 2000 is Sudarsky, Burrows and **Pinto** (not Hubeny); Hubeny is on the 2003 paper only.
2. Ultra-hot Jupiter threshold of about 2200 K is a **dayside** temperature in Parmentier et al. 2018, not Teq. Drop the "[M]" and cite it.
3. KELT-9 b: add Teq about 4050 K (Gaudi 2017); the report only gives the 4600 K dayside value.
4. Class boundaries of Sudarsky: the "~800 K" comes from the 2003 paper, the others from the 2000 paper; the scheme is stated in Teff (2000) and Teq (2003), not one consistent variable. See table.
5. TOI-849 b: 39.1 (+2.7/-2.6) M_Earth is the Armstrong 2020 value; "40,8" was not found in the abstract (not verified, may come from a later paper).
6. Chthonian: the Lecavelier des Etangs et al. 2004 A&A 418, L1 letter is titled "Atmospheric escape from hot Jupiters" and never uses the word "Chthonian". The term belongs to Hébrard et al., astro-ph/0312384.
7. Hot Jupiter occurrence: Wright 2012 gives 1.2 +/- 0.38 %, which the abstract says is more than double the Kepler and OGLE-III rates. "~1 %" is fine, but Howard 2012 does not state a hot-Jupiter rate in its abstract (it gives 0.013 +/- 0.002 planets per star for 8-32 R_Earth, P < 50 d).
8. Mocquet 2014 does exist (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 372, 20130164); the report's remark that the attribution is "probably wrong" is about a link to TOI-849 b, which this check neither supports nor refutes.
9. WASP-107 b JWST: Dyrek 2024, Sing 2024 and Welbanks 2024 disagree on CH4 (not detected in Dyrek, 4.2 sigma in Sing, 5 sigma in Welbanks). The report's "methane depleted by ~3 orders of magnitude" needs a source note (Sing: log abundance -6.03 +/- 0.21).
